練習問題 7 (1)

\(\displaystyle \dfrac{1}{\sqrt5-\sqrt2}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt5+\sqrt2}\)

分母はどちらも \(5-2=3\)。

\[ \begin{aligned}(\text{与式})&=\dfrac{\sqrt5+\sqrt2}{3}+\dfrac{\sqrt5-\sqrt2}{3}\\&=\dfrac{2\sqrt5}{3}\end{aligned} \]

答

練習問題 7 (2)

\(\displaystyle \dfrac{1}{3-\sqrt7}+\dfrac{1}{3+\sqrt7}\)

分母はどちらも \(9-7=2\)。

\[ \begin{aligned}(\text{与式})&=\dfrac{3+\sqrt7}{2}+\dfrac{3-\sqrt7}{2}\\&=\dfrac{6}{2}=3\end{aligned} \]

答

練習問題 7 (3)

\(\displaystyle \dfrac{1}{\sqrt7-\sqrt3}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt7+\sqrt3}\)

分母はどちらも \(7-3=4\)。

\[ \begin{aligned}(\text{与式})&=\dfrac{\sqrt7+\sqrt3}{4}+\dfrac{\sqrt7-\sqrt3}{4}\\&=\dfrac{2\sqrt7}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt7}{2}\end{aligned} \]

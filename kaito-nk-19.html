練習問題 2 (1)

\(2x^2+3x-1=0\)

\(a=2,\ b=3,\ c=-1\)。\(b^2-4ac=9+8=17\)。

\[ \begin{aligned}x&=\dfrac{-3\pm\sqrt{3^2-4\cdot2\cdot(-1)}}{2\cdot2}\\&=\dfrac{-3\pm\sqrt{17}}{4}\end{aligned} \]

答

練習問題 2 (2)

\(x^2-3x-1=0\)

\(a=1,\ b=-3,\ c=-1\)。\(b^2-4ac=9+4=13\)。

\[ \begin{aligned}x&=\dfrac{-(-3)\pm\sqrt{(-3)^2-4\cdot1\cdot(-1)}}{2\cdot1}\\&=\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt{13}}{2}\end{aligned} \]

答

\[ \begin{aligned}x&=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{1^2-4\cdot3\cdot(-1)}}{2\cdot3}\\&=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{13}}{6}\end{aligned} \]

練習問題 4 (1)

\(\displaystyle (2+\sqrt3)^2\)

\(\square=2\)、\(\bigcirc=\sqrt3\)。

\[ \begin{aligned}(\text{与式})&=4+4\sqrt3+3\\&=7+4\sqrt3\end{aligned} \]

答

練習問題 4 (2)

\(\displaystyle (\sqrt7-\sqrt2)^2\)

\(\square=\sqrt7\)、\(\bigcirc=\sqrt2\)。\(2\times\sqrt7\times\sqrt2=2\sqrt{14}\)。差の平方なので真ん中は「\(-\)」。

\[ \begin{aligned}(\text{与式})&=7-2\sqrt{14}+2\\&=9-2\sqrt{14}\end{aligned} \]

答

練習問題 4 (3)

\(\displaystyle (3\sqrt2-\sqrt5)^2\)

\(\square=3\sqrt2\)、\(\bigcirc=\sqrt5\)。\((3\sqrt2)^2=18\)、\(2\times3\sqrt2\times\sqrt5=6\sqrt{10}\)。

\[ \begin{aligned}(\text{与式})&=18-6\sqrt{10}+5\\&=23-6\sqrt{10}\end{aligned} \]

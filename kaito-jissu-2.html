練習問題 2 (1)

\(\displaystyle \sqrt{45}+\sqrt{20}-\sqrt{80}\)

\(\sqrt{45}=3\sqrt5\)、\(\sqrt{20}=2\sqrt5\)、\(\sqrt{80}=4\sqrt5\)。

\[ \begin{aligned}(\text{与式})&=3\sqrt5+2\sqrt5-4\sqrt5\\&=\sqrt5\end{aligned} \]

答

練習問題 2 (2)

\(\displaystyle \sqrt{72}-\sqrt{32}+\sqrt{8}\)

\(\sqrt{72}=6\sqrt2\)、\(\sqrt{32}=4\sqrt2\)、\(\sqrt{8}=2\sqrt2\)。

\[ \begin{aligned}(\text{与式})&=6\sqrt2-4\sqrt2+2\sqrt2\\&=4\sqrt2\end{aligned} \]

答

練習問題 2 (3)

\(\displaystyle \sqrt{75}-\sqrt{48}+\sqrt{3}\)

\(\sqrt{75}=5\sqrt3\)、\(\sqrt{48}=4\sqrt3\)。\(\sqrt3\) は \(1\sqrt3\) と見る。

\[ \begin{aligned}(\text{与式})&=5\sqrt3-4\sqrt3+\sqrt3\\&=2\sqrt3\end{aligned} \]
